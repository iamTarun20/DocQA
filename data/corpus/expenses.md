# Employee expense reimbursement

Northwind Labs reimburses reasonable business expenses entered in the Harbor ledger with the cost centre, purchase date, currency and business purpose. The employee's manager checks the purpose before Finance validates the supporting paperwork. Use the reimbursement flow for purchases made with personal funds. Company-card spending is reconciled in a separate queue even when the same merchant is involved.

Employees must submit expense claims within 14 calendar days of purchase. Receipts are mandatory for any individual reimbursement item of $25 or more, based on the billed amount before currency conversion. A bank alert is not an itemized receipt. If the vendor cannot provide one, attach the missing receipt declaration and record the date on which a copy was requested.

For shared meals, the person paying lists the team or meeting and any external attendees by role. Split mixed personal and business items into separate lines and enter only the business amount. A travel approval reference is required for trips with overnight accommodation. Finance returns a claim for correction if the cost centre does not match the reason given.

Approved claims are included in the next available reimbursement batch. When a returned claim is edited, the original submission timestamp remains visible to the reviewer. For a refund after payout, open a reversal entry against the original claim and attach the refund advice. Check the ledger status before submitting a duplicate or asking the merchant to charge a different payment method.
