# Sick leave and return to work

The Northwind Labs sick leave program provides 9 paid workdays each year to full-time staff. Notify the duty manager before the start of your scheduled workday when practicable. Record the absence in Compass when you return or ask People Operations to enter it on your behalf. Messages in a team channel are optional and should focus on coverage for the day.

A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive workdays of sick leave. Nonworking days between scheduled shifts do not restart the count. Send any certificate through the private People Desk attachment form. The manager receives the anticipated return date and any operational restrictions. The medical note stays in the People Operations case.

Employees can use a half day for an appointment that cannot be booked outside working hours. If a shift includes fieldwork or a lab booking, ask the scheduler to transfer the booking rather than cancel it from a phone. Staff who feel unsafe operating equipment should speak to the lab coordinator before resuming their normal shift.

A return conversation is scheduled after an absence that lasted at least a full workweek. The meeting is meant to identify practical adjustments such as lighter duties or changes to meeting times. Keep any return plan in the private case and agree a review date with the employee. Payroll corrections are made in the next regular cycle once the ledger is updated.
