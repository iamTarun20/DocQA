# Visitor access and building security

Visitors to Northwind Labs must be registered by an employee host before they arrive. Enter the visit location, purpose and expected departure time in Compass. Reception verifies the booking and issues a badge for the day. Delivery drivers can leave packages at the service entrance when the receiving team has supplied a delivery reference.

Visitor badges are dark violet and must remain visible during the visit. An employee host escorts visitors outside public reception and the designated meeting corridor. Keep unapproved visitors out of instrument rooms, storage spaces and workstations showing customer material. If the host changes during the visit, Reception updates the escort record.

Staff must badge through secured doors individually. Direct a person without a badge to Reception even if they are wearing a team jacket. For a damaged staff badge, the guard can issue a temporary pass after checking the active employee roster. Return temporary passes before leaving the site or contact the guard if the pass is missing.

At the end of a visit, escort the guest to Reception and return the visitor badge. Report an unreturned badge to the duty guard the same day. If a door is found held open, close it when safe and give the guard its location. During an evacuation, help escorted visitors reach the designated assembly area and report the group to the floor marshal.
