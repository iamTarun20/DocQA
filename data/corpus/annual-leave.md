# Annual leave

Northwind Labs grants full-time employees 23 paid annual leave days per calendar year. The People Operations ledger credits the allowance on January 2, and new starters receive a prorated balance on their first working day. Annual leave is recorded in half-day units. Holidays on the published office calendar do not draw down the balance, even when they sit inside a longer trip.

Employees can carry over up to 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Carried days must be used by March 31, and the booking system consumes them before the new allocation. A supervisor can move a booking past March only if People Operations records a company-directed cancellation. The monthly balance digest lists carried days separately to make the expiry visible.

Ask the line manager for approval in Compass before buying nonrefundable tickets. For a trip of more than 10 working days, include a one-page handover naming a backup for customer commitments and recurring approvals. A manager should answer within 4 working days or delegate the request while away.

If a booking is cancelled, update Compass and remove the calendar entry in the same sitting. A lead cannot convert an approved vacation into standby time without the employee's agreement. When an office closes unexpectedly during leave, People Operations reconciles the affected day in the following ledger export. Questions about a discrepancy go to the People Desk with the booking reference and the date of the balance snapshot.
