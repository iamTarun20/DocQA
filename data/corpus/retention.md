# Records retention and disposal

Northwind Labs assigns a retention category when a business record is stored. Owners use the approved location for the category and record the event that starts the retention period. A shared file should have one owning team even when several departments use it. When a team is reorganized, transfer ownership before the old workspace is archived.

Unsuccessful job applicant records are kept for 180 days after the hiring decision. The category covers interview notes, application materials and scoring sheets. A successful applicant's employment records move to the staff record category. Do not keep separate interview copies in personal folders after the hiring case is complete.

A legal hold pauses normal disposal of affected records. The hold notice identifies the relevant subjects, dates and storage locations. Records already scheduled for deletion must be removed from the disposal queue when a hold arrives. When a hold is lifted, review any remaining business or legal requirement before releasing the records for disposal.

Owners document the disposal date, record category and method in the retention log. Approved exports are subject to the same schedule as the source when they contain the same information. An archived workspace can be restored for a documented review, and its owner must either renew the archive or schedule it for removal when the review ends. Ask Records Operations about conflicting schedules before discarding the material.
