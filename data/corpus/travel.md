# Business travel

Arrange business travel through the Northwind Labs booking desk after the cost centre owner approves the itinerary. Enter the meeting dates, destination and planned travel days in Harbor. The booking desk holds reservations for a short time while approval is pending. Travelers should double-check the destination office and local dates before the hold expires.

Rail is the default for a trip with a scheduled rail journey of 4 hours or less. The standard hotel cap is $165 per night before local occupancy taxes. The travel desk can approve a higher rate for a sold-out event area or an accessibility requirement. Record the approval in the trip case before confirming the reservation.

Use flexible fares when an external meeting is likely to change, and compare the difference with the expected cancellation fee. Choose ground transport appropriate for the number of passengers and equipment carried. Private rental upgrades are paid by the traveler unless the desk records a business justification. A visitor itinerary should include the reception location and a working team contact.

If a carrier cancels a journey, contact the booking desk before accepting a replacement that adds an overnight stay. Keep the cancellation notice and any alternate itinerary for the expense claim. Managers should review the cost of postponing a meeting before authorizing repeat travel. When a traveler joins a trip midway, create a separate itinerary rather than editing a colleague's reservation.
