# Laptop and equipment inventory

Northwind Labs assigns an inventory tag to each laptop, test device and portable instrument before handover. The equipment coordinator records its custodian, location and condition in Compass. A team can lend a spare device for a short trip when the temporary custodian is recorded. Return the loan device to the issuing location or arrange a transfer with the coordinator.

Report a lost or stolen company laptop to the Service Desk within 45 minutes of discovering the loss. Provide the last known location, travel context and approximate time, without contacting a suspected thief. The Service Desk starts the device lock procedure and alerts the security duty analyst if an exposure is possible. A recovered laptop should be checked before it reconnects.

When equipment moves between sites, the sending coordinator records the courier tracking reference in the inventory case. The receiving coordinator inspects the packaging and confirms the tag. Report damaged packaging before assigning the device to a new user. Changes of location that occur on the same floor can be recorded during the next inventory update.

Equipment that is no longer safe or supported goes through the approved retirement process. Return accessories with a loan unless the coordinator authorizes replacement. For data-bearing devices, a wipe confirmation must be linked before disposal. The inventory retains the original tag and retirement decision so later warranty questions can be traced.
