# Wellness benefit reimbursement

Northwind Labs provides each eligible employee an annual wellness allowance of $480. The balance follows the calendar year and does not roll forward. Approved activities include fitness memberships, group exercise classes and ergonomic assessments. An allowance cannot be transferred between coworkers or taken as cash. Check the remaining balance in Harbor before committing to a long subscription.

Submit the invoice and proof of payment in the wellness reimbursement category. The invoice should describe the activity and its billing period. If a service covers several people, submit the portion attributable to the employee. A prepaid class pack can be claimed once the provider confirms payment and access to the classes.

Standard household purchases, meals and travel to a venue are outside the wellness category. A medical treatment claim should go through the separate benefits route. If an item might qualify as workplace equipment instead, ask the workplace coordinator to check the business purchase policy. Using the correct category prevents unnecessary review and keeps balances accurate.

A refund that affects an approved wellness claim should be recorded in the original case. If a membership changes provider, keep the previous invoice for any months already claimed. Employees can ask the benefits reviewer for an eligibility check before paying. The reviewer records the decision and the reason so later claims for the same service can be handled consistently.
