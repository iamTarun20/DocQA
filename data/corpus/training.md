# Mandatory learning and certification

Northwind Labs assigns learning by role through the Fieldguide portal. The learning coordinator checks whether the current role is reflected in the assignment before an overdue item is escalated. Site-specific modules may be added when an employee begins using new equipment. A manager can request equivalent credit by submitting the course outline and completion record.

Employees must complete security induction training within 21 calendar days of starting employment. The course covers suspicious login prompts, safe document sharing and incident reporting. A starter who cannot reach Fieldguide should ask the Service Desk for help while the course is still open. Finishing the reading without passing the final exercise leaves the assignment incomplete.

Courses with practical work include a demonstration checked by a qualified observer. The observer records the date and whether the employee can repeat the task independently. Staff should book refresher sessions before a certification expires. Access to restricted equipment can be paused when the associated practical training is overdue.

Fieldguide sends the employee and manager a monthly summary of open assignments. Managers can correct department or role information through People Operations. Keep an external certificate in the approved learning record instead of sending it to a shared inbox. If a course is replaced, the learning coordinator tells employees whether their existing credit carries over.
