# Vendor risk intake and review

Northwind Labs reviews suppliers that process company information, connect to company systems or operate a business-critical function. The business owner starts the intake in Harbor before signing an agreement. Describe the intended access and the locations where information will be processed. Procurement checks that the submitted scope matches the commercial order.

High-risk vendors must complete a follow-up assessment within 90 days of onboarding. The follow-up checks that promised controls are operating and that initial exceptions have owners. A contract renewal triggers a separate review of the current service scope. A lower-risk vendor can be reassessed if its access expands or a new dependency becomes essential.

For a service that handles Restricted records, Security Operations reviews isolation and access administration before production use. The legal reviewer checks notification obligations and the process for deleting records at contract end. The business owner should document what data remains in the company if the vendor becomes unavailable. Any response that depends on vendor support should name the escalation channel.

Keep the final decision and open conditions with the contract reference. Do not forward a vendor's security questionnaire to another supplier. If an assessment finds an urgent issue, Procurement informs the service owner and opens a mitigation case. Closing that case requires evidence from the vendor or a recorded change to the company's use of the service.
