# Parental leave and transition planning

Northwind Labs provides eligible primary caregivers 14 weeks of paid parental leave. The entitlement applies to eligible birth, adoption and permanent placement events. People Operations confirms eligibility and the leave start date in a private case. Employees should check the local supplement for additional leave or notice rules that apply to their employment location.

When a placement date is known, submit a planned leave request to People Operations and tell the line manager which work needs a handover. Use approximate dates when the arrival cannot be scheduled. People Operations can adjust the plan once the date is confirmed. The manager receives scheduling information and should keep any supporting paperwork in the private case.

The handover names owners for customer deadlines, budget approvals and equipment bookings. Managers should clear recurring meetings from the employee's calendar during the leave period. Employees can choose whether to receive team announcements. Any contact about a change to their role should go through the agreed channel.

Before the employee returns, arrange a conversation about working hours, site access and any refresher training needed. If a managed device has been inactive, allow time for its updates before an external meeting. The first week should include a recap of project changes and a review of urgent commitments. Record agreed adjustments and set a date to check whether they are still needed.
