# Acceptable use of work systems

Northwind Labs provides managed accounts and equipment for work, learning and coordination with approved partners. Limited personal use is allowed during breaks if it does not interfere with scheduled work, consume shared lab capacity or create a security concern. Business files should stay in an approved company workspace even when the employee has a more convenient personal account.

Install browser extensions from the catalog maintained by the Service Desk. A request for an uncatalogued extension should describe the business need, the permissions requested and any information the extension can read. If a plugin is needed for a single project, include the date it can be removed. Software trial accounts must use the project's approved billing and identity process.

Do not make customer or employee records available through a public sharing link. Check link settings again after moving a file between team spaces. Posting private material to an external transcription or conversion site requires a review. A coworker who receives an unfamiliar share should verify its owner before accepting it.

Staff should send suspected misuse to the Service Desk or their supervisor with the time and application involved. Preserve the relevant activity details if doing so is safe. The owner of the affected service reviews the report with the duty team and records the follow-up. When permissions are temporarily restricted, the ticket should explain how access can be restored.
