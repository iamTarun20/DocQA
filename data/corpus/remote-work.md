# Remote work and office scheduling

Hybrid employees at Northwind Labs can book up to 8 remote days per calendar month. A half day of remote work counts as half a day against the allowance. Staff based at the main campus usually attend the office on Tuesday and Thursday for planning and equipment handovers. Teams that rotate lab coverage can agree different core days with the site scheduler.

Book a remote day in Compass by 17:00 local time on the preceding working day. The calendar sends the team a coverage summary without showing home addresses. Inform your lead directly if a shipment or rehearsal needs a change at shorter notice. Unused remote days expire at the end of the month and cannot be exchanged for leave.

The normal remote location is a private workspace with a managed device and access to the corporate network tunnel. Use a headset when a discussion could be overheard. Printed lab records remain at the office, and screens displaying customer diagrams should be locked before leaving the desk. A remote arrangement from another country requires a separate People Operations review.

A supervisor checks shared coverage at the start of each week. If several bookings conflict with a visitor event or an instrument move, confirm the swaps directly with the people involved and leave a clear note on the team schedule. A short disruption to home internet can be handled by moving the session or using an available campus desk.
