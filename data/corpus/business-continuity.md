# Campus business continuity plan

Northwind Labs keeps a continuity plan for the main campus and a smaller copy at the reception desk. The duty coordinator reviews the plan when building access, utilities or an essential service is disrupted. Site leaders first account for people and then decide which operations can continue. The plan's activation does not authorize anyone to reenter an unsafe building.

The designated main-campus assembly location is Cedar Annex. Floor marshals check in with the site coordinator there and report any people they could not account for. A visitor host should stay with their guest until the headcount is complete. If the normal route is blocked, use the marked alternate route and notify a marshal.

The continuity team records which services need an alternate workspace and which can wait until normal access returns. Leads check the needs of active customer commitments, lab samples and scheduled deliveries. Reserve spare desks through the temporary site coordinator. Employees should keep business records in their approved workspaces during a relocation.

After an exercise or real activation, the site coordinator files a short timeline and any defects in the procedure. Update the handover copy if a route or contact has changed. The coordinator checks that reception and floor marshals have received the revision before retiring the previous version. New duty leads review the plan during their first coverage shift.
