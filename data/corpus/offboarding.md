# Staff departure and offboarding

When an employee leaves Northwind Labs, their manager opens a departure case with the agreed final working day and handover owner. The handover lists customer contacts, shared assets, scheduled work and approvals that must be reassigned. People Operations confirms the end time privately with the manager and the employee. The Service Desk uses that confirmation to coordinate removal.

Remove standard application access by 18:00 local time on the employee's final working day. If the departure requires immediate action, People Operations sets a separate removal time. The application owner checks group memberships and privileged access as well as the primary account. Any change to the schedule must be recorded in the departure case.

Arrange return of the laptop, security key and badge through the equipment coordinator. Staff working away from the campus can use an approved collection service. The coordinator confirms the inventory tags when the items arrive. Employees should transfer business records to the assigned owner before returning equipment.

Keep the mailbox and approved business records according to the applicable retention category. A manager may request delegated access through a recorded approval path. The case remains open until equipment and account tasks have owners or documented exceptions. People Operations runs a closure check and sends unresolved items to the relevant team queue.
