# Purchasing and supplier quotes

Start a Northwind Labs purchase request in Harbor before agreeing to price or delivery dates with a supplier. Describe the requirement, the cost centre and the proposed owner. The request should include a proposed delivery location and any timing constraint that rules out a standard supplier. Procurement checks for an existing catalog item before opening a new purchase order.

Purchases estimated at $4,000 or more require at least 2 written supplier quotes. Compare the total cost including delivery and service fees. An exception for a single-source part requires a procurement lead's written approval in the request. Do not divide related orders to fit below the quote requirement. An extended warranty counts as part of the total when purchased with the equipment.

A requester records arrival in Harbor after inspecting quantity and condition. If delivery is partial, receive only the units that actually arrived. Send mismatched invoices back to Procurement and keep the delivery paperwork with the order. A requester should never tell a vendor that an internal approval is complete before a purchase order has been issued.

For recurring consumables, Procurement may establish a standing order with a defined monthly ceiling. Review usage before extending a standing order to another office. Procurement records the comparison and selected supplier so a substitute can be found later. When a unit price changes, keep the original quote attached and ask Procurement to update the order rather than rewriting the quote history.
