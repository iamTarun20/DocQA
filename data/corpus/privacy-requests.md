# Privacy request intake

Northwind Labs accepts privacy requests through the People Desk or the external privacy mailbox. The intake reviewer opens a restricted case and records the received date, request type and jurisdiction when known. A request forwarded by another team keeps the original received date. The reviewer acknowledges receipt and describes the next step without confirming details about a record.

Verify the requester using 2 approved identity attributes before releasing records. Use the minimum information needed to match the relevant account. For a representative, check the authority to act as well as the identity of the requester. Store the verification outcome in the case and avoid copying full identifiers into the working notes.

Assign each system search to a role that can retrieve records without expanding permissions. Keep search terms and dates in the restricted case so the result can be reproduced. The reviewer checks for third-party information and applies required redactions before preparing the response. A second reviewer verifies that the export matches the approved scope.

Deliver the response through the approved secure channel and document the delivery date. If verification cannot be completed, record the attempt and explain what is needed to continue. A requester may narrow the scope during intake. Preserve the original request and the approved change so the case reflects what was requested and delivered.
