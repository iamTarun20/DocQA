# Company procurement cards

Northwind Labs issues procurement cards to staff who make approved operating purchases. The cardholder acknowledges the conditions before activation and records each transaction in Harbor. Enter the business purpose and attach an itemized receipt even if the card statement already shows the merchant. A cardholder must keep the card in their own custody.

The standard procurement card limit is $750 per transaction. Ask Procurement to raise the limit for an approved purchase that needs a card. The request should include the amount, approval reference and date when the temporary limit can be removed. Splitting a purchase into separate charges to avoid the limit is prohibited.

Cardholders reconcile monthly statements by the fifth working day of the following month. Match refunds to their original charges and flag any amount that cannot be explained. If a purchase is disputed, tell Finance before opening a duplicate reimbursement claim. The cardholder's manager reviews the reconciliation and checks that unclear charges are assigned an owner.

Report a missing card to Finance and the issuing bank as soon as it is discovered. Keep any alert or case number with the last known transaction. When a cardholder changes teams, the new cost centre owner reviews whether the card is still needed. Cards that are no longer needed are cancelled and recorded in the account inventory.
