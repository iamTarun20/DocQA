# Laboratory safety and instrument handover

Northwind Labs staff must complete site orientation before using a laboratory workbench independently. The lab coordinator confirms which instruments require practical sign-off. Keep the bench clear of personal bags and position power leads away from wet work. If the task introduces a new material, review its handling instructions with the coordinator before bringing it into the shared space.

An approved orbital shaker run can be left unattended for up to 90 minutes when the vessel is secured and the instrument timer is set. Record the expected finish time and a contact in the bench log. Before leaving, confirm that the instrument is stable at the selected speed. Tasks outside the approved settings need an operator in the room.

If an instrument behaves unusually, stop it if safe and mark it unavailable in the booking calendar. Leave a short note describing the symptom and recent setup. Do not ask another operator to repeat a suspected unsafe condition. The coordinator reviews the case with maintenance before allowing another booking.

At handover, remove samples and return reusable tools to their marked locations. Dispose of waste in the stream listed for the material. The incoming operator checks the work area and records any issue before starting. When an incident interrupts a run, preserve the bench log and inform the duty coordinator.
