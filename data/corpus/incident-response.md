# Security incident response

Report a suspected security incident in the Northwind Labs duty queue. Include the time, system name and symptoms, and ask the duty analyst to acknowledge it. Keep original messages, logs and screenshots in the incident workspace. Where possible, preserve evidence before restarting a device or changing its configuration. For an immediate safety issue, follow the building's emergency procedure first.

The duty analyst must start SEV-1 incident triage within 15 minutes of the initial alert. SEV-1 means a confirmed or credible threat to production availability, restricted records or critical lab operations. Open an incident channel, name an incident commander and record a working severity in the timeline. The commander selects owners for containment, evidence collection and communications.

Containment actions should have a rollback path or explicit commander approval. Record each change with its time and owner so a second responder can verify the effects. After containment, confirm that representative workflows are healthy and that any temporary restrictions have a removal plan. A monitoring alert returning to normal is only one piece of the recovery check.

Within 5 business days after closure, the commander schedules a review of the event sequence. The review separates confirmed facts from hypotheses and assigns action items to named roles. Keep legal or customer-sensitive detail in the restricted appendix. The standard closure template links to the owner of each follow-up so the team can check progress without reopening the incident.
