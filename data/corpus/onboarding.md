# New starter onboarding

The hiring manager opens a Northwind Labs starter case at least 5 working days before the arrival date. The case reserves a device, a desk and a badge pickup slot. People Operations sends a calendar itinerary once Facilities confirms the office location. A remote starter receives a delivery window instead of the equipment pickup appointment.

Each new employee is assigned a Navigator for their first week. The Navigator is a peer who demonstrates everyday workflows, introduces the duty contacts and helps the employee find the right documentation. The Navigator does not approve permissions or expense requests. A manager names a substitute in the starter case if the assigned peer becomes unavailable.

On arrival, the employee checks their badge, signs in on the managed laptop and confirms they can reach the People Desk. The first-week checklist, START-17, is due by 16:00 local time on Friday. Open items can be annotated with a case reference. A starter should record a failed device setup even if an engineer has already offered to investigate it.

The manager holds a brief recap before the employee's first independent assignment. The recap covers who handles urgent incidents, how to ask for a review and which meetings require preparation. The starter then picks a short workflow to demonstrate back to the team. People Operations archives the checklist only after the manager acknowledges the outstanding actions and gives them a due date.
